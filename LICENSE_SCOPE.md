## Legacy artwork and media excluded from MIT

The image collections in `assets/art_v2/`, `assets/xianxia/`, `assets/unused/`,
and screenshots/videos in `docs/showcase/` are **not covered by the MIT grant**.
The maintainer identifies the legacy artwork as AI-generated, primarily with
Gemini and GPT Image. File-level model/account/input-reference provenance has
not been fully established. This release makes no new downstream reuse grant
for those images or media. Their exclusion does not assert copyright over
material that is not copyrightable under applicable law.

The prior 2D version remains available in source, but MIT licensing of its code
does not clear its excluded artwork for commercial reuse. Use independently
cleared replacement art if adapting that version. Current desktop release ZIPs
are built from an allowlisted 3D-only staging project and omit these collections.
Development scratch files in `.superpowers/` are also excluded from this grant
and from release packages.

AI assistance does not create rights in third-party material or guarantee that
every output is copyrightable or unique. This license grants only rights held
by the licensor; applicable third-party rights remain unaffected. Provenance
records are in [docs/ART_V2.md](https://github.com/AmethystineAlpaca/moonlit-jianghu/blob/v0.4.1-preview.1/docs/ART_V2.md).

notices. The removed Photoroom outputs remain in historical Git objects.
