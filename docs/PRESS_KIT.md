## Short description — English

Stillwater is a 3D wuxia action chapter built with Godot 4.6.2. Fight through a rain-washed courtyard, dash away and recall through opponents, shove enemies into each other, and turn deflections into openings. This single-map preview is seeking feedback on combat response, enemy readability and movement.

## 简介 — 中文

《雨歇》是《月下江湖》当前的 3D 武侠动作篇章，使用 Godot 4.6.2 制作。在雨后山院中破除三处封印，迎战无相：闪避留影后回锋穿斩，以破阵击退制造碰撞，借见切与破势寻找反击机会。当前为单地图试玩版，重点征集操作响应、读招和战斗反馈方面的意见。

## What to show

- **Movement becomes an attack route:** a dash leaves a temporary echo; Q recalls through enemies along the return path. Buildings can block the path.
- **Positioning creates follow-ups:** E shoves enemies into architecture or other enemies. Timed frontal guards and the shove can reflect projectiles; broken posture opens an F follow-up.
- **Three weapon rhythms:** a balanced straight sword, an interrupting heavy blade, and a faster spirit blade whose third combo hit sends a projectile.
- **A compact courtyard journey:** clear three lantern seals, choose two upgrades and face a two-phase boss among stone, water, maple trees and rain.

Ordinary enemy attacks use weapon preparation and a brief pre-strike glint. Area boundaries are reserved for actual area attacks. The current audio emphasizes combat Foley; there is no continuous background-music loop.

## Preview boundaries

The preview contains one map. It is under active development and still needs broader human playtesting, dedicated character art, and cross-hardware validation. The recorded baseline passed 32 regression scripts on 2026-09-06; automated checks do not establish player satisfaction or release quality. macOS builds are not Apple-notarized. Windows hardware and physical-controller validation remain pending.

The older **2D Qinglan Night** version remains separately launchable in the repository. Its screenshots and spell systems belong to that earlier version and should be identified as such when used in retrospective coverage.
